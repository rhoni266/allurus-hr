Version 2026-10-29 · Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)

Draft. This notice is a summary while the full notice is finalised. Questions or requests: hello@allurus.my.

What we collect

For the company admin who signs up: name, work email, mobile number and company details. For staff the company adds: the details the company enters, such as name, contact details, IC number, salary, bank account, attendance and check-in location and selfie.

Why

To provide the HR, attendance and payroll service the company uses, to sign people in securely, and to bill the company. We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who decides

For staff data, the company is the data user under the PDPA and Allurus processes the data on its behalf. Staff should ask their company to see or correct their data.

Where it is kept

On Google Cloud (Firebase), protected by sign-in and access rules so each company sees only its own data.

How long

While the company uses Allurus, and up to 90 days after its subscription ends, unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

You may ask to see or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@allurus.my.
