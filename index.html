The problem

Five tools. Five spreadsheets. Nobody sees the same numbers.

Most growing SMEs run on a patchwork. Each department keeps its own records, and the owner spends month-end stitching them together.

Today

Every department keeps its own version.

payroll_final_v3.xlsx Stock book WhatsApp approvals Accounting software Sales notebook
  • Payroll is rebuilt from timesheets and WhatsApp messages every month.
  • Stock is counted in one book, ordered from another, paid from a third.
  • Sales, cash and costs only meet in a spreadsheet, weeks later.

With Allurus

One source of truth.

  • A number is entered once and reaches every department that needs it.
  • Purchases, stock, jobs, payroll and invoices are linked end to end.
  • You see cash, sales and costs as they happen, not at month-end.
