## A 10-minute walkthrough

1. **HR admin**: on the dashboard, point out *Focus for today*. In **Time off**, approve a request and show the approval chain and the Terengganu calendar. In **Schedule**, show the week's shifts. In **Payroll**, choose *Run payroll*: EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB and HRD Corp are worked out, then the statutory and bank files and the EA forms.
2. **Employee**: on Home, show *Check in with selfie* (the face and location checks need a phone and HTTPS). Request leave with an MC photo, then open **Payslips** and **Claims → scan a receipt**.
3. **Manager**: under **Approvals**, show Daniel's request waiting, and who else in the team is off that day.
4. **Auditor**: open last month under **Reports to verify** and choose *Verify*. The month locks, and the trail records who did it.

**To show a request arriving live**, use two tabs in the same browser: sign in as HR admin in the first tab. Open the same address in a second tab (it opens as HR), choose **Sign out**, then **Employee**. When Daniel requests leave in the second tab, it appears on HR's screen in the first within a second. Signing out in one tab doesn't sign out the other.

Different browsers or devices each keep their own demo copy and don't see each other. For a live demo across a laptop and a phone, use the cloud version: register a company with the sample team and invite a second email of your own.
