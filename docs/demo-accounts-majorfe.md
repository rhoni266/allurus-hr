# Demo accounts: Major Far East Sdn Bhd

Open **https://rhoni266.github.io/allurus-hr/?demo=majorfe**. You can also open `?demo` and choose **Major Far East** on the sign-in card. Either click a role under **Explore the demo company**, or type an email below with the demo password.

**Demo password:** the same one as the Seri Maju demo, given in [`demo-accounts.md`](demo-accounts.md). It is stored only as a hash.

Major Far East Sdn Bhd is a made-up wholesaler of hardware and building materials, with a warehouse in Kepong, Kuala Lumpur:
- 12 staff, working Monday to Friday, on the Kuala Lumpur holiday calendar;
- 8 stock items, bought from vendors and sold to contractors and hardware shops;
- two months of attendance, leave, claims and payroll.

It uses only the stock and purchasing part of Operations, plus the forklift and lorry under Machines. It has no production and no deliveries. All client and vendor names are invented.

## What the supervisor sees when a purchase is made

When a purchase order is approved, Hafiz gets a notification in the employee app, for example *Coming in: PO-2610-007, Mon, Nov 2 · Jubin Seramik Perak: 590 box floor tiles. Get the store ready.*

Tapping it opens that purchase order straight away: the vendor, the items and quantities, the expected date and who approved it. The app switches him to his stock view for this; he doesn't have to find the page.

On his phone's Home there is also a **N orders coming in** button. The stock page starts with a **Coming in** list: every order on its way, soonest first, with any that are late or only partly delivered marked.

He can receive goods against an order and count stock. He can't see the books, payroll or other staff records.

## A 5-minute walkthrough

1. **Admin (Lim Kok Leong), on a computer.**
   - The bell shows a purchase order from purchasing (Chong Wei Jie) waiting for him.
   - Open **Stock & purchasing → Purchase orders** and choose *Approve*. The message says the store has been told.
   - Safety gloves are below their reorder level: on the **Stock** tab, use *Make PO*, then approve it. As the owner, he can approve his own orders.
2. **Supervisor (Hafiz), on a phone or a narrow window.**
   - He starts in the employee app. The bell has the new *Coming in* notifications.
   - Tap one: the purchase order opens in his stock view.
   - On **Stock & purchasing**, *Coming in* lists everything on its way, including the rebar order that came in short.
3. **HR admin (Farah Nadia).**
   - Show leave, attendance and payroll.
   - Her menu has no Operations and no books.
